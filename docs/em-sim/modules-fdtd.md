# EM simulation – module map: the full-wave engine

Part of [modules.md](modules.md): `lib/emsim/fdtd/` (framework-free, no DOM, no `node:` imports), the 3D FDTD solver of
D5 built by M6 ([fullwave.md](fullwave.md) has the decisions, [notes-M6.1.md](notes-M6.1.md) what M6.1 built). Arrays
follow the spike's layout: E = [Ex | Ey | Ez], H likewise (stored as η0·H), cell c = i + nx·(j + ny·k), ghost node 0,
PEC walls on node planes 1 and n − 1. A step that adds or renames a file here updates this table.

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `model.ts` | M6.1: the structure as 3D geometry, mm, z as stackZ: `buildModel(setup, 'trace' \| 'via', { trMin, length, z0, eeff })` → `Model` (`cu` copper layers `CuLayer` with `zb`, `zt`, `sheet` and its plane `z`, `plane` with `openings`, `shapes` (`Shape`: `rect`, `disk` annulus); `cyl` barrels and return vias; `drilled`; `outerTop`/`outerBottom` with their mask; `dielectric`; `erMax`, `trMin`, `hmax` (λ/15); `extent`, `open`; the mesh's `fixed` lines by rank, `spans` (count rules), `blocks` (barrel blocks); `ports` (`PortSpec`: axis, pos, dir, across, centre, zSig, `ref` plane or coplanar gaps, z0 and εeff from the 2D solver, `feed` L, `planes`); `path` for τrt; `margins`; `notes`); `column(m, variant)` the εr/σ pieces in z (`V_TOP`, `V_BOTTOM`: outer copper under mask, `V_DRILL`), `variantAt`, `inLayer`, `inShape`; `largestCell`, `feedLength`; constants `HMIN` 0.02, `RATIO` 1.3, `COURANT` 0.95, `CPML_CELLS` 8, `BARREL_CELLS` 6, `TR_MIN` 35 ps, `F_TAN` 1 GHz, `C0_MM`, `ETA0` |
| `mesh.ts` | M6.1: `buildAxis(AxisSpec)` → `AxisMesh` (`lines` incl. CPML, `cpml` cells per side, `fixed` taken, `dropped`, `moved`, `gaps` with `n`, `nmin`, `relaxed`), `buildMesh(model)` → `Mesh` (`x`, `y`, `z`, `hmax`, `notes`), `gridOf` (ghost node first), `minCell`. Fixed lines by rank, ≥ HMIN apart; each gap the fewest cells from its count rule up that fit within RATIO of its neighbours' end cells (a mix of the least and largest such sequences), sweeps to a fixed point; a count rule yields (`relaxed`) only where RATIO and HMIN forbid it |
| `materials.ts` | M6.1: `buildMaterials(model, mesh, dt, Float32Array \| Float64Array)` → `Materials` (`ca`, `cb` 3N, `cut`/`frac` the conformal edges, `pec` counts, `variants`): z exact by prefix integrals of the columns (`prepColumn`, `colInt`), across on 4 × 4 samples per dual face; tangential: arithmetic over the height, harmonic along the edge; normal: harmonic along, arithmetic across; σ parallel arithmetic, series ε²·mean(σ/ε²); PEC by intervals of each mesh line through the copper (closed outlines), conformal fraction = length outside / length |
| `cpml.ts` | M6.1: `buildCpml(model, mesh, dt, A)` (M6.2a: through `cpmlOf(mesh, dt, A, erAt)`, which the harness's boxes call) → `Cpml` (`x`, `y`, `z` profiles `CpmlAxis`: depth, σ at εr 1, κ, α at E nodes and H half nodes; `erNode`/`erHalf`; `coef` b, c tables (side slabs per position and z node/half, σmax/√εr of the layer); `psi` 4 arrays per open slab; `slabCells`); `cpmlAxis`, `cpmlBC`, `slabCellsOf`, `tableEntries`; `CPML_ORDER` 3, `KAPPA_MAX` 4, `ALPHA_MAX` 0.05, `EPS0` |
| `ports.ts` | M6.1: `pulseOf(trMin)` → `Pulse` (f_run = 1.52/tr, σ, t0 = 5σ), `pulseAt`; `buildPorts(model, mesh, dt, ca, cb)` → `Port[]` (`R` = Z0·paths, `paths`, `len`, per edge `edges`, `src`, `aR`; CA, CB of the path edges set with the resistor; `v` probes at each plane's two lines, `i` loops between them, `lines`) |
| `estimate.ts` | M6.1: `courantDt`, `runTime(trMin, path)` (T = 10σ + 3τrt), `estimate(EstimateIn)` → `Estimate` (cells, slab cells, Δt, steps, `memory` per buffer and total, largest buffer, seconds at a rate); `verdicts(est, Env, limits)` → refusals and warnings of §11.4 and the coarser-mesh `Offer`; `RATES`, `OVERHEAD` 1.5, `RING` 64, `RECORD_PER_PORT` 6, `MiB` |
| `problem.ts` | M6.1: `buildProblem(setup, kind, { precision, closed, loads, env, rate, force, allocate, …ModelOptions })` → `Built` (`ok`, model, mesh, estimate, verdicts, offer, notes, `problem`: grids, Δt, `lines` (c0Δt/(κΔ), the spike's six 1D arrays), materials, CPML, ports, pulse, zeroed `E`, `H`, `record`); nothing allocated when refused; margins widened until ports are ≥ 5 cells from the CPML (`portCellsToCpml`); `allocatedBytes` |
| `ref.ts` | M6.1: `RefKernel(problem f64 closed, sources)`: `run(steps)` (H with E·f on the conformal edges, E, port 1's source at (n + ½)Δt), `energy()` (Σ εr·f·V_E·Eⁿ·Eⁿ⁺¹ + Σ V_H·H̃², f the conformal fraction since M6.2a) |
| `cpu.ts` | M6.2a: the time loop ([notes-M6.2a.md](notes-M6.2a.md)): `CpuLoop(grid, { sources, monitorEvery (100), sourceEnd, stopDb (50), maxSteps })` on a `Grid` (the fields of a `Problem` it needs; the harness builds boxes by hand), in the problem's precision, in place: `run(steps)` → steps done (stops when `status` leaves `'running'`: `'settled'` W −50 dB under its maximum after `sourceEnd`, `'unstable'` W negative, not finite or 2× its least after `sourceEnd`, `'done'` at `maxSteps`), `measure()` one step that returns W, `step`, `energy` [step, W][], `wMax`; one step: E·f on the conformal edges, H, H's ψ, E restored, E (W summed in the same pass on monitor steps), E's ψ, sources at (n + ½)Δt; kernels compiled per instance by `new Function` from their own source; `Source` {idx, w, wave(t)}, `portSources(problem, drive)`, `pulseEnd`, `MONITOR_EVERY`, `UNSTABLE_RISE` |
