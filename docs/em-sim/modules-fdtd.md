# EM simulation – module map: the full-wave engine

Part of [modules.md](modules.md): `lib/emsim/fdtd/` (framework-free, no DOM, no `node:` imports), the 3D FDTD solver of
D5 built by M6 ([fullwave.md](fullwave.md) has the decisions, [notes-M6.1.md](notes-M6.1.md) what M6.1 built). Arrays
follow the spike's layout: E = [Ex | Ey | Ez], H likewise (stored as η0·H), cell c = i + nx·(j + ny·k), ghost node 0,
PEC walls on node planes 1 and n − 1. A step that adds or renames a file here updates this table.

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `model.ts` | M6.1: the structure as 3D geometry, mm, z as stackZ: `buildModel(setup, 'trace' \| 'via', { trMin, length, z0, eeff })` → `Model` (`cu` copper layers `CuLayer` with `zb`, `zt`, `sheet` and its plane `z`, `plane` with `openings`, `shapes` (`Shape`: `rect`, `disk` annulus); `cyl` barrels and return vias; `drilled`; `outerTop`/`outerBottom` with their mask; `dielectric`; `erMax`, `trMin`, `hmax` (λ/15); `extent`, `open`; the mesh's `fixed` lines by rank, `spans` (count rules), `blocks` (barrel blocks); M6.2b: a trace section's signal edges by the **thirds rule** (`thirdsOf`: lines h/3 inside and 2h/3 outside, h = w/(`TRACE_CELLS` + 2/3); option `thirds: false` puts lines on the edges) and its trapezoid on the copper faces (`Taper` `tx` of a rect, `taperAt`; `inShape`/`inLayer` take z); `ports` (`PortSpec`: axis, pos, dir, across, centre, zSig, `ref` plane or coplanar gaps, z0 and εeff from the 2D solver, `feed` L, `planes`); `path` for τrt; `margins`; `notes`); `column(m, variant)` the εr/σ pieces in z (`V_TOP`, `V_BOTTOM`: outer copper under mask, `V_DRILL`), `variantAt`, `inLayer`, `inShape`; `largestCell`, `feedLength`; constants `HMIN` 0.02, `RATIO` 1.3, `COURANT` 0.95, `CPML_CELLS` 8, `BARREL_CELLS` 6, `TR_MIN` 35 ps, `F_TAN` 1 GHz, `C0_MM`, `ETA0`; M6.4b: `EDGE_INSET` (1/8): a thirds-rule trace's copper is its rect inset by `EDGE_INSET`·h on each side (the lines stay at the true edges' thirds) |
| `mesh.ts` | M6.1: `buildAxis(AxisSpec)` → `AxisMesh` (`lines` incl. Fixed lines by rank, ≥ HMIN apart; each gap the fewest cells from its count rule up that fit within RATIO of its neighbours' end cells (a mix of the least and largest such sequences), sweeps to a fixed point; a count rule yields (`relaxed`) only where RATIO and HMIN forbid it |

| `cpml.ts` | M6.1: `buildCpml(model, mesh, dt, A)` (M6.2a: through `cpmlOf(mesh, dt, A, erAt)`, which the harness's boxes call) → `Cpml` (`x`, `y`, `z` profiles `CpmlAxis`: depth, σ at εr 1, κ, α at E nodes and H half nodes; `erNode`/`erHalf`; `coef` b, c tables (side slabs per position and z node/half, σmax/√εr of the layer); `psi` 4 arrays per open slab; `slabCells`); `cpmlAxis`, `cpmlBC`, `slabCellsOf`, `tableEntries`; `CPML_ORDER` 3, `KAPPA_MAX` 4, `ALPHA_MAX` 0.05, `EPS0` |
| `ports.ts` | M6.1: `pulseOf(trMin)` → `Pulse` (f_run = 1.52/tr, σ, t0 = 5σ), `pulseAt`; `buildPorts(model, mesh, dt, ca, cb)` → `Port[]` (`R` = Z0·paths, `paths`, `len`, per edge `edges`, `src`, `aR`; CA, CB of the path edges set with the resistor; `v` probes at each plane's two lines, `i` loops between them, `lines`) |
| `estimate.ts` | M6.3: `CALIB_WARMUP` 10, `CALIB_STEPS` 30, `CALIB_MIN_MS` 50 and `CALIB_MAX_STEPS` (a GPU times as many more steps as fill 50 ms, twice, the faster counts), `CALIB_MARGIN` 1.1, `rateFromCalibration(loopRate)` (the estimator's rate, so the estimate is the calibrated time × 1.1). | `problem.ts` | M6.3: `limitsOf(model, mesh)` (the offer's limits), `judge(built, env, rate)` (§11.4 again at a calibrated rate or another backend's environment), `allocateBuilt(built, opt)` (a problem built with `allocate: false`). | `ref.ts` | M6.1: `RefKernel(problem f64 closed, sources)`: `run(steps)` (H with E·f on the conformal edges, E, port 1's source at (n + ½)Δt), `energy()` (Σ εr·f·V_E·Eⁿ·Eⁿ⁺¹ + Σ V_H·H̃², f the conformal fraction since M6.2a); M6.4b: the faces of `hcut` divide their H update by A (H = s + (H − s)·(1/A)), and `energy()` weighs them by A·V_H |
| `cpu.ts` | M6.2a: the time loop ([notes-M6.2a.md](notes-M6.2a.md)): `CpuLoop(grid, { sources, monitorEvery (100), sourceEnd, stopDb (50), maxSteps })` on a `Grid` (the fields of a `Problem` it needs; the harness builds boxes by hand), in the problem's precision, in place: `run(steps)` → steps done (stops when `status` leaves `'running'`: `'settled'` W −50 dB under its maximum after `sourceEnd`, `'unstable'` W negative, not finite or 2× its least after `sourceEnd`, `'done'` at `maxSteps`), `measure()` one step that returns W, `step`, `energy` [step, W][], `wMax`; one step: E·f on the conformal edges, H, H's ψ, E restored, E (W summed in the same pass on monitor steps), E's ψ, sources at (n + ½)Δt; kernels compiled per instance by `new Function` from their own source (with a serial since M6.2b, so V8's compilation cache does not share their feedback); `Source` {idx, w, wave(t)}, `portSources(problem, drive)`, `pulseEnd`, `MONITOR_EVERY`, `UNSTABLE_RISE`, `UNSTABLE_FLOOR` (M6.2b: a rise counts only above −30 dB of the maximum); M6.3: the stop and stability rules as `judgeEnergy(EnergyWatch, step, W, dt, …)`, shared with the GPU loop (same numbers); M6.4b: the faces of `mat.hcut` likewise after H's ψ, the monitor's correction V_H·(A − 1)·H² |
