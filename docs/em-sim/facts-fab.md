# EM simulation – facts: JLCPCB and KiCad (research of 2026-11-16, step M0.2b)

facts.md §10 and §11, split off to keep facts.md under 40 kB. What JLCPCB publishes about its stackups, materials and
impedance sheet, and which fields KiCad writes for the stackup and the net classes. Keys in `code` are entries of
`references.bib`; what each source may be cited for is in [sources.md](sources.md). The owner's boards and JLCPCB's
numbers for them are cases C38–C45 in [facts-cases.md](facts-cases.md).

**Dates.** Every JLCPCB number below was read on **2026-11-16** from the URL named beside it. JLCPCB changes its pages
without notice (the calculator guide says its values are "for reference only" and "may be adjusted in the future"), so
the presets of M1.1 carry this date and URL, and a later step re-reads them before a release.

### 1.2 Naming of stackups (U2, naming-scheme image)

`JLC 06 16 1 1 - 3313 G` = **JLC**, layer count (06), board thickness (16 = 1.6 mm), external copper (1 = 1 oz;
2 = 2 oz), internal copper (H = ½ oz, 1 = 1 oz, 2 = 2 oz), prepreg type next to the outer layers (3313), then a variant
letter. So JLC061611-3313G is a 6-layer 1.6 mm board, 1 oz outer and **1 oz inner**, with 3313 prepreg under the outer
layers, variant G.

| Core (one value) | 4.6 | U1 (older single value; U2's table supersedes it) |

| Solder mask | 3.8 | U1 ("CEr"), U2, U3 |

**Frequency: none of U1–U4 states the frequency at which these εr apply, and none gives a loss tangent.** The
calculator presumably uses them as frequency-independent constants. Open (notes-M0.2b.md): NP-155F's datasheet (linked
from G) gives εr at stated frequencies; reading it is for M1.1 or phase 2 (losses). KiCad's own default reference
frequency is 1 GHz (§2.3).

### 1.6 Copper

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Outer, 1 oz, in the stackup | 0.035 mm | U1, G (API "LineThickness":"0.035mm") |
| Outer, 1 oz, in the calculator's parameter table | **1.6 mil (0.0406 mm)** | U2 ("External copper thickness (1 oz)") |
| Outer, 2 oz | 70 µm; not supported by the calculator | U4; U2 |
| Inner ½ oz | 0.0152 mm (U1, G); 0.6 mil (U2) | U1, U2: "internal layers have a slightly smaller thickness … due to … copper being lost in production during deoxidation" |
| Inner 1 oz | 0.03 mm (G); 1.2 mil = 0.0305 mm (U2) | G, U2 |

**Base foil and plating are not given separately** on any page: 0.035 mm is a finished outer thickness for the
stackup drawing, while the calculator's table says 1.6 mil. Which one the calculator's field solve uses is open; the
two differ by 0.5 Ω on the owner's microstrip (C39).

### 1.7 Solder mask

U1 (calculator parameters C1, C2, C3, CEr) and U2 agree: **1.2 mil (0.0305 mm) on laminate ("above substrate", "base
soldermask"), 0.6 mil (0.0152 mm) over copper, 1.2 mil between traces, εr 3.8.** U3: "Solder mask ink thickness:
≥ 10 μm" (a process minimum, not the model). These map directly onto D8's three mask thicknesses (engine.md §2).

### 1.8 Trace shape, spacing and the impedance sheet

- **Trapezoid:** U2's parameter table: "Trace top width = Trace base width − 0.7 mil" (17.8 µm narrower in total,
  8.9 µm per side, independent of copper thickness). In Polar's terms (W1 = base, W2 = top) this is W2 = W1 − 0.7 mil.
- **Which width the sheet and the calculator mean:** not stated. The owner's sheet column is "Trace Width (mil)"; the
  calculator returns a "trace width". Read as the **base (design) width** W1, the width in the Gerber files, since the
  top is derived from it (assumption, open for the owner to confirm with JLCPCB if it matters: 0.7 mil is 13 % of
  5.42 mil).
- **Spacing:** "Conductor Spacing: The gap between the two conductors in a differential trace" (U2); "Increasing
  conductor spacing increases the calculated trace width". Where it is measured (base or top) is not stated; taken as
  edge to edge at the base, as engine.md §3 does.
- **Coplanar:** "Conductor-to-Ground Gap: The gap between the signal conductor and the surrounding ground in a coplanar
  waveguide configuration" (U2); the sheet's types are "Single Ended (Non coplanar)", "Differential Pair (Non
  coplanar)" and coplanar variants (owner's sheets).
- **Ranges and tolerance:** the calculator accepts "20 to 90 Ω for single-ended and 50 to 150 Ω for differential
  signals" (U2); impedance tolerance ±10 %, "±5 % … upon special request" (U3); the sheet's column is
  "Impedance(ohm)±10%".
- **Sheet form** (owner's `Required_impedance_control.xlsx`, JLCPCB's template): Signal Layer (L1…L6) · Reference Layer
  · Impedance Type · Trace Width (mil) · (unnamed) · Trace Spacing (mil) · Impedance (ohm) ±10% · Position (a
  screenshot). JLCPCB's engineers answer with their own widths and spacings in an image table and keep or name the
  stackup (C39–C42).
- **Solver and Zdiff definition:** **not documented** by U1–U4 (no solver is named; Polar is not mentioned). The
  calculator offers only **symmetric** edge-coupled pairs, and for a symmetric pair definitions A, B and D of
  facts.md §5 all give 2·Zodd, so the definition does not matter for the owner's cases. For the theory: "JLCPCB's
  calculator reports a differential impedance of symmetric pairs, Zdiff = 2·Zodd under every definition in use".

### 2.2 The `(stackup …)` block (inside `(setup …)`)

```
(stackup
  (layer "F.SilkS" (type "Top Silk Screen") (color "White"))
  (layer "F.Paste" (type "Top Solder Paste"))
  (layer "F.Mask" (type "Top Solder Mask") (color "Green") (thickness 0.01))
  (layer "F.Cu" (type "copper") (thickness 0.035))
  (layer "dielectric 1" (type "prepreg") (color "FR4 natural") (thickness 0.092)
         (material "FR4") (epsilon_r 4.3) (loss_tangent 0.02))
  … (layer "In1.Cu" (type "copper") (thickness 0.03)) (layer "dielectric 2" (type "core") …) …
  (copper_finish "None") (dielectric_constraints yes))
```
(the owner's carrier, re-indented; both boards' stackups are identical).

| Token | Where | Meaning, units, versions |
|---|---|---|
| `layer "NAME"` | each item, top to bottom | a board layer name (`F.SilkS`, `F.Paste`, `F.Mask`, `F.Cu`, `In1.Cu`…, `B.Cu`, `B.Mask`, `B.Paste`, `B.SilkS`) or `"dielectric N"` (N = 1, 2, … top down) |
| `type "…"` | every item | free text written by KiCad: `"Top Silk Screen"`, `"Top Solder Paste"`, `"Top Solder Mask"`, `"copper"`, `"core"`, `"prepreg"`, `"Bottom Solder Mask"`, `"Bottom Solder Paste"`, `"Bottom Silk Screen"` (`KEY_CORE`, `KEY_PREPREG`, `KEY_COPPER` in `stackup_predefined_prms.h`) |
| `color "…"` | mask, silk, dielectric | a name ("Green", "FR4 natural") or `#RRGGBBAA`; not physics |
| `thickness T [locked]` | mask, copper, dielectric | mm; `locked` only on dielectrics (kept when the board thickness is recomputed) |
| `material "…"` | dielectric, mask | free text ("FR4", "Not specified") |
| `epsilon_r x`, `loss_tangent x` | written only when the item has a material | dimensionless; the owner's masks have no material, so **no mask εr in the file** |
| `addsublayer` | inside a dielectric item | starts the next sublayer of the same dielectric; it is followed by that sublayer's `color`, `thickness`, `material`, `epsilon_r`, `loss_tangent`. All versions 6–master |
| `spec_frequency f`, `dielectric_model constant\|djordjevic_sarkar` | dielectric (sub)layers | **master only (11.0 development, 20260511)**: εr's reference frequency in Hz and the model |
| `copper_finish "…"` | end of block | e.g. "None", "ENIG", "HAL SnPb" |
| `dielectric_constraints yes\|no` | end | "impedance controlled" box of the stackup dialog |
| `edge_connector yes\|bevelled`, `castellated_pads yes`, `edge_plating yes` | end, only when set | `castellated_pads` is written by 6.0–9.0's stackup writer, not by 10.0's |

Writer differences 6.0 → 10.0 beyond the table: 6.0–9.0 print `epsilon_r` with `%g`, 10.0 with `FormatDouble2Str`
(same value, maybe more digits); 9.0 moved to `FormatBool` (`yes`/`no`). A reader should accept all of them and ignore
unknown tokens (M1.1).

### 2.3 KiCad's defaults (the placeholders engine.md §2 flags)

`BOARD_STACKUP_ITEM` (6.0 and 10.0 alike): a new dielectric gets material **"FR4", εr 4.5, tanδ 0.02** ("for FR4"); a
mask gets εr **3.3** (`DEFAULT_EPSILON_R_SOLDERMASK`) and the default mask thickness (0.01 mm, the owner's value);
silkscreen εr 1.0. Master adds a reference frequency of **1 GHz** (`SetSpecFreq(1e9)`) and the constant model. The
owner's 4.3 / 0.02 on every dielectric with material "FR4" is also a placeholder (a template's or the owner's, not
JLCPCB's): **any εr of exactly 4.5 or 4.3 with tanδ 0.02 and material "FR4" on every layer is to be flagged**.

### 2.4 Net classes

- **KiCad 5:** `(net_class …)` items in the `.kicad_pcb`. **6.0 and later:** moved out of the board file
  (`KiCadSexprPcb`: "The net class section has been moved out of the board file") into the `.kicad_pro` JSON under
  `net_settings`.
- `net_settings.meta.version` is the schema: **6.0 writes 2, 7.0 and 8.0 write 3, 9.0 writes 4, 10.0 and master 5**
  (constant `netSettingsSchemaVersion` per branch). Migrations: 0→1 overbar syntax; 2→3 class `nets` lists become
  `netclass_patterns`; 3→4 adds `priority`, `netclass_assignments` values become arrays (a net may have several
  classes); 4→5 adds `tuning_profile`.
- **Class object** (`net_settings.classes[]`), lengths in mm: `name`; `clearance`, `track_width`, `via_diameter`,
  `via_drill`, `microvia_diameter`, `microvia_drill`, `diff_pair_width`, `diff_pair_gap`, `diff_pair_via_gap`
  (PCB); `wire_width`, `bus_width`, `line_style`, `schematic_color`, `pcb_color` (schematic and colours); `priority`
  (9.0+; Default has 2147483647); `tuning_profile` (10.0+). In 6.0–10.0 a field
  of a non-default class is **written only when set**; a missing field falls back to the `Default` class (the owner's
  `50R_SE` has only `name`, `priority`, `track_width`, `tuning_profile`).
- **Membership:** 6.0 `classes[].nets` (list of net names); 7.0+ `netclass_patterns` (`{"netclass", "pattern"}`,
  wildcards and regular expressions, e.g. `/DAC/DCLK(N|P)`) and `netclass_assignments`.
- Also in `.kicad_pro`: `board.design_settings.track_widths`, `via_dimensions` (`diameter`, `drill`),
  `diff_pair_dimensions` (`width`, `gap`, `via_gap`) — the user's pre-defined sizes, first entry 0 = "use net class";
  and `rules` (`min_track_width`, `min_via_diameter`, …). Clearances to pours and antipads may come from the
  `.kicad_dru` custom rules (the owner's do: C44, C45), which phase 1 does not read.
